VulnMart: progress for the guide meeting

VulnMart is an online marketplace that is intentionally vulnerable. Each person gets a private copy, attacks it to practise the OWASP Top 10 weaknesses (or to be assessed by a recruiter), and every successful attack is detected and scored automatically.

source: README.md, initial.md

What we are making, whether it is achievable, and who is doing it

  • What we are making: a practice and hiring platform with 11 challenges, built by three developers.
  • It is achievable: the first parts already run and are checked with real commands (see "What already runs").
  • It is being implemented by three people: Lab, Platform and Target, each with a task list in weekly sprints. Final submission: first week of November 2026.

How it works in six steps

  1. Sign up or accept an invitation. A learner signs up on the VulnMart website. A candidate is invited by a recruiter and accepts the invitation.
  2. Get a private copy of the shop. The Lab starts one separate copy of the shop just for that person, with its own data, so nobody can disturb anybody else.
  3. Attack the shop. The shop looks like a real online marketplace and contains 11 deliberate weaknesses (challenges). Each one hides a secret value called a flag that is different in every copy.
  4. The attack is noticed. A watcher next to the shop records what happens and recognises a successful attack by itself. Pasting the flag is the backup way to prove it.
  5. The attempt is scored. The Lab scores the attempt automatically, using the challenges solved, the time taken and the hints used.
  6. Results are shown. The learner sees progress and hints. A recruiter sees the score, the time per challenge and the technique used. The private copy is then reset or removed.
