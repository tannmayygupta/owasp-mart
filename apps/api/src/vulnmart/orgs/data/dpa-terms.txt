Data-processing terms for {{company}}

These terms apply when {{company}} invites candidates to an assessment on VulnMart.

Roles
- {{company}} decides why and how the results of its candidates are used. {{company}} is the controller of that data.
- VulnMart runs the assessment and stores the candidate's data for {{company}}. VulnMart is the processor of that data.

What VulnMart does
- It processes candidate data only to run the assessment, to show the results to {{company}} and to keep the records the candidate was told about.
- It does not use candidate data for anything else, and it does not show it to another company.
- It keeps candidate data for the time shown to the candidate and then deletes it. It carries out a candidate's request to see, download or delete their data, and tells {{company}} where the request touches its results.
- It keeps the data safe with the measures described on the VulnMart site, and it tells {{company}} without undue delay if it learns that candidate data was exposed.

What {{company}} does
- It invites only people who are 18 or over, and only for a real hiring purpose.
- It uses the results only for that purpose, and does not share a candidate's data outside {{company}} except where the law requires it.
- It answers its candidates' questions about how it uses their results.

Accepting these terms is needed before {{company}} can invite candidates. The acceptance is recorded with the name of the person who accepted, the version of these terms and the time.
